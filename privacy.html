Privacy Policy

GmailOrganiser is a personal desktop application used to organise Gmail accounts through the Google Gmail API.

Data Access

GmailOrganiser accesses Gmail data only after the user authorises the application through Google's OAuth authentication system.

Use of Information

Information accessed through Gmail is used solely by the application for the user's personal Gmail organisation activities.

Data Storage

The application runs locally on the user's computer. Gmail data is not intentionally transmitted to or stored on a separate server operated by GmailOrganiser.

Third Parties

GmailOrganiser does not sell, rent, or share Gmail data with third parties. Google processes data according to Google's own policies.

Contact

GmailOrganiser is a personal-use application and does not operate a public customer support service.
